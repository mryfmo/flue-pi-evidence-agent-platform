## Behavior

The CLI reads existing ledgers without changing their formats:

- `.orchestration/tasks/<task_id>.md`
- `.orchestration/orchestrator/events.jsonl`
- `.orchestration/{reports,validation,sandboxes,acceptance}/<task_id>*`
- `.orchestration/orchestrator/leases.json`
- `artifacts/audit/*.jsonl`
- `artifacts/telemetry/traces.jsonl`

Missing sections are printed as `missing`; zero records exits 1. Body-like
fields such as prompt/content/text/stdout/stderr are replaced with SHA-256
digests.
