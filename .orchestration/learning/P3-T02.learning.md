# P3-T02 Learning

## Reusable Learning
- OPA 0.70.0 accepts `import rego.v1`, `if`, and `contains`, and Homebrew OPA 1.x parses the migrated policies without `--v0-compatible`.
- `opa test policy -v` can exercise packages and JSON data together when the policy directory includes `policy/tenants.json`.
- `opa build -b policy --revision <git-sha>` writes the revision into the bundle manifest; `opa inspect` verifies it.

## Reflected In
- Rego v1 policy syntax.
- `scripts/opa-test.mjs` and `scripts/opa-bundle.mjs`.
- `docs/VALIDATION_PLAN.md` and `docs/POLICY_MODEL.md`.
